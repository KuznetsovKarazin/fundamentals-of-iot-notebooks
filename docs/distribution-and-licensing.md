# Distribution and licensing scope

This repository distributes the computational companion to *Fundamentals of the Internet of Things*. It separates executable teaching material from the manuscript and publisher-managed teaching resources.

## Companion material

The existing [`LICENSE`](../LICENSE) covers source-code cells and utility scripts under MIT, unless stated otherwise. [`CONTENT_LICENSE.md`](../CONTENT_LICENSE.md) covers the repository's non-code educational material under CC BY-NC 4.0, unless stated otherwise. Preserve attribution and any file-specific notices when reusing or adapting material.

A link to a third-party repository, publication, dataset, or service does not relicense it. Its own terms still apply. Synthetic data must remain identified as synthetic when reused.

## Material outside this repository

The textbook manuscript, chapter PDFs and LaTeX sources, publisher-provided assets, and instructor slide decks are not distributed as part of this notebook package. This repository's licenses do not grant rights in those materials. Reuse or distribution of them remains subject to their applicable publisher permissions and terms.

Public availability of a companion notebook does not imply permission to upload the book itself or to redistribute separate instructor resources. Any future ancillary archive should identify its contents and licenses clearly and keep book content separate.

## Access and versions

Repository access and copyright permission are different matters: a private repository requires access even where a file carries a reuse license. Colab links based on GitHub may therefore require authorization while the repository remains private. The dated Drive snapshot offers an alternative download route according to its sharing settings.

Record the exact repository commit or release when citing computational work. Do not treat a mutable Drive copy or the `main` branch as an archival version.
